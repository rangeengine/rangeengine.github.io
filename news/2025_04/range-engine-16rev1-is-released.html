Range Engine 1.6 Rev 1 Is Released!

13 April 2025

The Range Engine 1.6 LTS just got even better! We've just released our first improved version, full of tweaks and improvements that makes the difference.

Some of the improvements and new features:

  • Cascaded Shadow Maps

    A crucial technique for rendering high-quality shadows in large-scale environments, balancing performance and detail across varying distances from the camera.

  • PostFX Improvements

    We've enhanced the post-processing effects. SSR has a better quality with Binary Search, now the game engine uses Alchemy AO with a performance improvement up to ~60%, Tonemaps now have Saturation and Temperature, and more advanced optimizations.

  • Screen Space Irradiance

  • Vignette

  • Exponential Height Fog and Mist Improvements

  • Debug Mode Improvements

    Now you can move, rotate and scale objects with a in-game manipulator, And also change the object color and transparency.

See all the changes with gifs and examples in the Release Notes
